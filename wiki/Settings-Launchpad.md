![Settings · Launchpad](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/reiy-leo/side-dock/images/settings-en-launchpad.png)

Reads Launchpad folders **read-only** (name plus the apps inside, in Launchpad order), one row per folder:

- **"Add to…"** — appends the folder's apps to the end of a Dock bar (existing icons stay put)
- **"Replace…"** — clears a Dock bar and fills it with the folder's contents

Only the target bar is written; apps already pinned to the native Dock aren't added twice; apps that can't be found (deleted from disk) are skipped and reported. After changing folders in Launchpad, click "Refresh".

> From macOS 26 the system replaces Launchpad with the Apps view, so this page shows a note and is disabled.
