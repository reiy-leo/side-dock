![Settings · Desktops](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/reiy-leo/side-dock/images/settings-en-desktop.png)

On the left is the desktop list **grouped by display** (rename in place, **up to 10 characters**, stored locally only — macOS 15 has no API for naming desktops; group titles are display names and refresh when displays are plugged in or out). There's also a "Show name" switch, a position (top / middle / bottom) and a style (**Default / Flowing Neon / Cyber Violet**) for the large 1-second name shown when you switch. "Default" is a frosted glass panel; "Flowing Neon" and "Cyber Violet" are animated backgrounds (a sweeping light band and neon outline, same text) that animate only during that second.
